3AI-Generated Documents

NexGenLegal uses a third-party large language model API to generate document drafts based on your inputs. Important points about this process:

  • Document prompts and generated content are processed through the API solely to produce your requested output
  • We do not use your document content to train any AI model
  • Generated documents are provided "as-is" and must be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before use — NexGenLegal is a drafting aid, not a substitute for legal advice
  • You retain ownership of the documents you generate and any content you input
